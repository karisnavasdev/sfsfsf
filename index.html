FAQ

Good questions.

This is a static demo. It does not connect to Robinhood, and it never asks for a password, seed phrase, or private key.

How does a cash-out work?

You set an amount, a coin, a network, and your wallet. The screen issues an order code. On a live desk, dollars would be matched to that code and coins sent to the wallet. Here the steps stay in your browser.

Why sign in?

The sample profile keeps a member number, invite code, and orders together. Continue with Robinhood only stores that sample on this device. No Robinhood account is opened.

What is the balance?

The portfolio shows a sample welcome balance of $48. It is locked until October 4, 2026, 12:00 AM US Eastern, then the withdraw button explains that nothing is sent.

How much can I send?

The form accepts $2 to $20,000 per order. Those are interface limits for the demo, not a live settlement cap.

How long does a payout take?

Analytics shows sample timing. You can walk an order from waiting to completed yourself. Nothing is broadcast to a chain.

Can I send crypto back to Robinhood?

Not in this demo. Robinhood Chain → dollars is marked as opening later on Capital Flow.

Overview

Chain is a static HTML fork of a cash-out bridge, aimed at Robinhood Chain. You quote an order, and the page remembers it locally.

YouRobinhood USDDeskCoins on Robinhood Chain

Signing in

Continue with Robinhood writes a sample profile in local storage: a name, member number, invite code, and a $48 balance. It does not open Robinhood, collect a password, or keep an access token.

Cashing out

  1. Open the bridge and enter a dollar amount.
  2. Pick the coin and network, then paste your wallet. EVM routes expect a 0x address.
  3. Create the order. You get a code like CH-7K2Q9M.
  4. Use the demo controls to move the status forward.

The quote is fixed when the order is created. Stablecoins are one-to-one with the dollar, minus the fee.

Fees & limits

The quote uses 2% plus $0.50, taken from the amount you type. Limits on the form are $2 to $20,000.

Staying safe

  • This site never asks for a password, seed phrase, or private key.
  • Do not send money because a page looks like a cash-out desk.
  • Robinhood, Robinhood Chain, and related marks belong to their owners. This page is not affiliated with them.
